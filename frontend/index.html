Serverless Volunteer & Shift Management Web App

Most organisations within the health and social care sectors — such as those managing residential care homes or community outreach — struggle with coordinating shift workers and volunteers efficiently.


I created a serverless backend system using API Gateway, AWS Lambda (Python), and DynamoDB to track volunteer availability and log shift hours. By keeping it serverless, the organisation pays nothing when the system is idle overnight. I also created a frontend where new staff can be added, shifts can be logged manually and displayed immediately without having to use a CLI.
